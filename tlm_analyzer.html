TLM Contact Resistance Analyzer

Computes the resistance of each channel from its I–V data, then extracts sheet resistance (R_sh), contact resistance (R_C), transfer length (L_T) and specific contact resistivity (ρ_C) from a linear fit of R_total vs. L. Current is assumed to be in A (amperes) and voltage in V.

W = 4000 μm (fixed)

Use the switch on each Column to enable or disable it. For every enabled Column, enter voltage (V) [V] and current (I) [A] with the same number of points — there is no need for 101 points or the full 0–10 V range (e.g. you can enter only 0–1.5 V). At least 2 Columns must be enabled.
